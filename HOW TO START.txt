JOBAGENT - HOW TO START
=======================

JobAgent finds jobs, tells you which ones fit you, and writes a matching
resume and cover letter. It runs on your own computer and is free.

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 20 minutes, mostly waiting for downloads)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install Python:  https://www.python.org/downloads/
   Windows: on the FIRST screen of the installer, tick
            "Add python.exe to PATH", then click "Install Now".

2. Install Ollama (the free AI engine):  https://ollama.com/download

3. Unzip the JobAgent folder somewhere easy, like your Documents folder.

EVERY TIME YOU USE IT
---------------------
4. Open the JobAgent folder and double-click:
     Windows:  Start JobAgent.bat
     Mac:      Start JobAgent.command
               (first time only: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again)

   A black window appears. The very first time it sets things up for a few
   minutes. Then JobAgent opens in your web browser.

   KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you use JobAgent. Close it when you're done.

5. In the browser, follow the pages on the left, top to bottom:
     Setup        - download the AI model (one time)
     My profile   - upload your resume, check what it filled in, click Save
     Find jobs    - search job sites, or paste a job you found
     Your matches - your best matches, scored. Click "Resume + cover letter"
                    (or just one of them) and download the PDF or Word file
     Templates    - optional: paste in your own cover letter and resume
                    layout; JobAgent tailors them to each job

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Always read the resume and cover letter before sending them. If JobAgent
  shows a "Please check" note, look at those lines first.
- Your resume, profile and documents stay on your computer. Only job
  searches go out to the job sites you pick.
- If something goes wrong, take a screenshot of the black window and the
  browser page and send it to whoever shared JobAgent with you.
